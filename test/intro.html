Introduction to Xiang Yu

Early Life

Xiang Yu is a prominent figure in Chinese history, known for his role as a military leader and his rivalry with Liu Bang during the late Qin Dynasty. He was born in 232 BC and became famous for his exceptional bravery and strategic prowess on the battlefield. Xiang Yu's most notable achievement was his leadership in the Chu-Han Contention, where he fought against Liu Bang for control of China after the fall of the Qin Dynasty.
